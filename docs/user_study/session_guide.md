# User test session guide (about 45 minutes per colleague)

**Goal:** real evidence for the report's Impact section: how long it takes a working reporter to find
leads by hand versus with Scout, whether the app's leads hold up, and a quote.

**You need:** your laptop with the app running (`bash setup_and_run.sh`), the folder
`data/pdfs/` open in Finder, a timer, `results_sheet.csv`, and `feedback_form.md` (print it or share it).

Ask permission first: "Can I quote you by name and job title in my class report, and take screenshots
of the session?" Write down the answer in the results sheet.

---

## Part 1: By hand (20 minutes, timed)

Say this:
> "Here are 27 real Beaufort County records: capital project trackers and Council minutes from
> December 2020 to April 2022. Imagine you're on the county beat with an afternoon to find a story.
> Look for project costs that changed, or spending with no approval on record. Say each lead out loud
> when you find it. You have 20 minutes."

Record in the results sheet:
- the time (minutes:seconds) at which they name **each** lead, and what it was
- whether it matches a reference lead (`data/REFERENCE_LEADS.md`: L1 Arthur Horne, L2 EMS Station 31,
  L3 Boundary Street tennis courts, L4 the pool figures, L5 pavement survey)
- stop at 20:00 even if they are mid-search (note what they were doing)

Do not help or hint. Do not show the app yet.

## Part 2: With Scout (10 minutes, timed)

Say this:
> "Now the same records with the app. Pick a beat, press Find leads, and tell me what you'd do with
> each lead."

Have them operate the app themselves. Start the timer when they press the button. Record:
- time until the first lead card appears, and until they finish reading all cards
- for **each card**: would they pursue it? (yes / maybe / no) and is the evidence correct? (they can
  open the PDF to check)
- anything confusing on the screen
- also show them the **Dismissed** tab: do they agree with the reasons?

Take screenshots: their face is not needed; capture the screen with a card they found useful.

## Part 3: Feedback form (5 minutes)

Hand them `feedback_form.md`. Then ask the open question out loud and **write down their exact
words**: "If you were still on the beat, would you use this? What would you tell your editor about it?"

---

## Be honest in the report

- Part 1 always comes first, so the app run benefits a little from what they saw by hand.
  Say so in the report.
- With two colleagues, this is a small pilot, not a study. Report the actual numbers.
- Some reference leads take Council packets to confirm; a 20-minute search finding none of them is
  a real result, not a failure.
