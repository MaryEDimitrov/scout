# News values (from the journalist)

Use these to judge whether a lead is worth the journalist's time. When you tag a lead with news
values, use exactly these labels. "So what" is a test every lead must pass, never a tag.

- **Impact**: How many people are affected? The more people it touches, the bigger the news.
- **Conflict**: A struggle, a disagreement, or a problem that needs a solution.
- **Timeliness**: News must be new, or have a fresh development. Judge this against the dates of
  the documents, not today's date.
- **Proximity**: People care most about what happens on their own street or in their own town.
- **Prominence**: Actions involving well-known people, such as leaders, are more newsworthy.
- **Unusual**: Something out of the ordinary, or that breaks a pattern.
- **Currency**: Topics the public is already talking about.
- **Human interest**: The emotional angle; how people deal with challenges.
- **So what**: Why should the reader care? If you cannot answer this, the lead is weak.
- **Verified evidence**: The lead rests on facts that can be proven from the documents, never on
  gossip or guesses.
