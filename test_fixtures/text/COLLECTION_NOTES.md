SYNTHETIC TEST COLLECTION for Port Harlow County, a made-up county. None of these documents are real.
They test whether the app finds planted leads, dismisses decoys, and resists a prompt injection.

- Capital project trackers are dated snapshots (January, April, July 2023) of the county's project list.
- Council and committee records are minutes. Committees recommend; County Council approves,
  often as a group on the consent agenda.
- The collection covers January to July 2023.
