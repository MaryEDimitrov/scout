PORT HARLOW COUNTY PUBLIC FACILITIES COMMITTEE MINUTES - March 20, 2023
Members present: Committee Chair Dana Whitfield, Vice Chair Luis Ortega, Council Members Ames, Brooks, and Kline.
ACTION ITEMS
1. CHANGE ORDER #1 FOR RIVERSIDE LIBRARY ANNEX ($390,000)
The Facilities Director stated that soil testing found unsuitable fill under the annex footprint. Change Order #1 adds $390,000 for excavation and structural fill, increasing the Riverside Library Annex project from $850,000 to $1,240,000, funded from Library Impact Fees.
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Ames, seconded by Council Member Brooks, to approve Change Order #1 for the Riverside Library Annex and forward it to County Council. The motion passed without objection.
2. RECOMMENDATION OF AWARD - COUNTYWIDE PAVING YEAR 2
The County Engineer stated the low bid from Coastal Asphalt was $1,000,000. Staff recommends award of $1,000,000 plus a 15% contingency of $150,000, for a total of $1,150,000, funded from the C Fund.
Motion: It was moved by Vice Chair Ortega, seconded by Council Member Kline, to recommend award of Countywide Paving Year 2 for a total of $1,150,000 including contingency, and forward it to County Council. The motion passed without objection.
3. STATUS REPORT
The Facilities Director gave a status report on the Emergency Operations Center Roof, Oak Street Sidewalks, and Harbor Park Pavilion. For informational purposes only.
