PORT HARLOW COUNTY FINANCE COMMITTEE MINUTES - June 19, 2023
Members present: Committee Chair Renee Calloway, Council Members Ames, Ortega, and Pruitt.
1. FISCAL YEAR 2023 BUDGET AMENDMENT - FIRST READING
The Finance Director presented a budget amendment using year-end surplus for road repairs, employee raises, and a $275,000 construction overrun on the Harbor Park Pavilion.
Discussion: Committee Chair Calloway asked why the committee was not informed of the Harbor Park Pavilion overrun before it appeared in a budget amendment. Council Member Pruitt asked staff to provide the change orders behind the overrun at the next meeting.
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Ortega, seconded by Council Member Ames, to forward the budget amendment to County Council for first reading. The motion passed 3-1, with Committee Chair Calloway opposed.
