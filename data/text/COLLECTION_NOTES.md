Real public records from Beaufort County, South Carolina, covering December 2020 to April 2022.

- Capital project trackers are dated snapshots of the county's capital project list
  (construction, design, and in-development projects with cost, funding source, and status).
- Council and committee records are minutes and agendas of County Council and its committees
  (Public Facilities, Finance, Executive). Committees recommend; County Council approves,
  often as a group on the "consent agenda".
- Packet excerpts are selected pages of the backup material handed to Council for a meeting.
- Known gap: Council records from April to November 2020 are not in this collection.
  An approval from that period cannot be confirmed or ruled out.
- This is a curated selection, not every Council record from the period.
