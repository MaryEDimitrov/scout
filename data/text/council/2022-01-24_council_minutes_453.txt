a resolution to extend until March 16th, 2022 the prescribed time to apply for the Special Property Tax

which could apply to such taxpayers, and to allow these taxpayers 30 days from the date on which a
determination is made on their application to pay their taxes.

15. FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE TO AMEND ARTICLE IX. GOVERNMENT-OWNED PROPERTY AND
FACILITIES, DIVISION 2. RESTRICTED ACCESS
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Sommerville, Seconded by Council Member Flewelling that
County Council amend Article IX. Government-Owned Property and Facilities, Division 2 of the Beaufort
County Code of Ordinances to include a new section, Section 2-675 Camping Prohibited, as set forth in
today's agenda materials.
The Vote: The motion was approved without objection.
16. FIRST READING OF AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE 2020 BUSINESS LICENSE ORDINANCE
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Flewelling, Seconded by Council Member Dawson that County
Council amend Article 3 Section 2 of the 2020 Business License Tax Ordinance, so that the definition of
business means any business calling, occupation, profession or activity engaged in with the object of
gaining benefit or advantage, either directly or indirectly; in addition to the above described activities
constituting doing business in the county, an individual shall be deemed to be in business if that individual
owns and rents two or more residential units or holds a partial interest therein within the county,
excluding the municipalities therein; this applies to long term residents of thirty days or more only.
The Vote: The motion was approved without objection.

25. A RESOLUTION CREATING A CITIZENS ADVISORY COMMITTEE TO RECOMMEND POSSIBLE 2022
TRANSPORTATION SALES TAX PROJECT LIST AND/OR VALUE/ DURATION LIMITATION
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Howard, Seconded by Council Member Glover to approve a
resolution creating a Citizen's Advisory Committee to recommend possible 2022 transportation sales tax
project list an/or value duration limitation.
Discussion: Chairman Passiment referenced items listed in the resolution's attached documentation,
which included a requirement that Committee appointments be communicated to the Council Chairman
by a certain date. He then opened the floor for discussion on the submission date.
Council Member Cunningham commented on the April 29th deadline date referenced in the
documentation, and gained confirmation that the Committee has to be created before that date.

Council Member Howard proposed Friday, February 11th as the submission date.
Council Member Sommerville expressed his support for Council Member Howard's proposed date.
Council Member Dawson voiced his objection to the motion and commented on his preference to push
the decision about the resolution to 2024, given the failure of previous referendums.
Council Member Glover asked Council Member Dawson if he would still submit a name if the resolution
was passed. Council Member Dawson confirmed that he would still submit a name.
Chairman Passiment confirmed February 11th as the date to submit an appointee name. There was no
objection made to this statement.
Chairman Passiment made a statement as to why the Beaufort County Transportation Advisory Group was
not in this original resolution, as well as the importance of public input.
Council Member Rodman stated that he is in support of a Citizen's Advisory Committee but voiced his
opposition to the proposal due to the difficulty of a referendum given the short time period and the
sequential nature of the proposed tasks. Council Member Rodman provided alternative suggestions to
the proposal and referenced the benefits of a longer-term option to allow for the initiation of more
projects and the ability to pay for those projects over an extended period of time, of staff
recommendations provided at an earlier date, and of parallel discussions by the Citizen's Advisory
Committee, the Beaufort County Transportation Advisory Group, and County Council.
Council Member Sommerville made comments on the value of a penny in Beaufort County, the benefit of
the sales tax in terms of revenue collection from tourists, and the possible use of part of the penny tax
funds for land preservation purposes if the sales tax referendum passes.
Council Member Cunningham commented on issues with the previous referendum, his support of the
resolution and hope for expediency, his negative stance toward 5B, and preference for a shorter timeline.
Council Members Howard and Glover commented on accident occurrences and impacts.
To see the full discussion, please click the link below.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/153563
The Vote: Voting Yea: Chairman Passiment, Vice Chairman Sommerville, Council Member Glover, Council
Member Howard, Council Member Lawson, Council Member McElynn, and Council Member Cunningham.
Voting Nay: Council Member Hervochon, Council Member Rodman, Council Member Dawson, and Council
Member Flewelling. The motion passed 7:4.
