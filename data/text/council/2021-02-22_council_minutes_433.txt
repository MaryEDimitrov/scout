16. A RESOLUTION TO SET FORTH A UNIFORM POLICY FOR PROCESSING, RESPONDING TO, AND TRACKING
REQUESTS FOR PUBLIC RECORDS IN COMPLIANCE WITH THE FREEDOM OF INFORMATION ACT SC CODE OF
LAWS 30-4-10 et seq.
Motion: It was moved by Vice-Chairman Sommerville, Seconded by Council Member McElynn to approve a
resolution to set forth a uniform policy for processing, responding to, and tracking requests for public records
in compliance with the freedom of information act SC Code of Laws 30-4-10 et seq.
County Attorney Kurt Taylor and Deputy Attorney Brittaney Ward stated this policy was drafted in an effort to
keep some consistency in responses and create a system for county staff so that everyone is on the same page
and knows how to process and respond to requests.
To see full discussion please click the link below.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/113220
Council Member Flewelling requested the item be referred to the executive committee. Council Members
Hervochon, Cunningham, Lawson and Rodman spoke in favor of this request.
This item was referred back to the executive committee for further discussion.
