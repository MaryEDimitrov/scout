8. 2021-22 BUDGET AMENDMENT TO RECOGNIZE ARPA FUNDS- WHITNEY RICHLAND, DEPUTY COUNTY
ADMINISTRATOR
- Recommendation for Splash Pad/ Aquatic Facility for St. Helena has been removed and replaced with
two items 1) Partnership with the Gullah/Geechee Farmer Co-Op for $250,000.00 2) A generic use for
the remaining $1.25 Million for Recreational Facilities or improvements on St. Helena Island
- Recommendation by Administration to utilize $2.5 Million of ARPA funds to provide for premium pay
under the final rule. This would compensate employees who worked during the pandemic's height,
specifically between April 2020- January 2022. The total any one employee would receive will not
exceed $3,000.00. Final estimates are still being worked on. If the amount exceeds $2.5 Million, the
Administration’s recommendation is to use General Fund Surplus from each of the two fiscal years
completed during the pandemic.
- Council Member Hervochon requested the number of members that make up the Gullah/Geechee
Farmer Co-Op.
Discussion: To view the full discussion click the link below.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/154011
Motion: It Was Moved by Council Member Howard, Seconded by Council Member Passiment to Approve
the Recommendation to Amend The 2021-22 Budget to Recognize ARPA Funds as explained. The Motion
Was Approved Without Objection.
Status: Forward to County Council for Approval

9. RECOMMENDATION TO APPROVE CHANGE ORDER 2 TO MYRTLE PARK COMMERCIAL SITE
DEVELOPMENT (IFB 021121E) – JARED FRALIX, ASSISTANT COUNTY ADMINISTRATOR
- Beaufort County entered into an incentive agreement with CSD Myrtle Park, LLC to invest up to
$577,000 toward site development and utilities for Myrtle Park. Beaufort County approved a contract
award to JS Construction Service for Earthwork & Site Utilities at Myrtle Business Park Commercial
Development for $344,147 with a 10% contingency totaling $367,561.70.
- In addition to the original scope, asphalt paving is needed to improve the site. Negotiations with JS
Construction Services have yielded a package providing: a 6-8” rock base, 2” binder asphalt course,
and 1670 linear feet of concrete curbing, for a total of $180,000.00.
- The original contract minus contingency totals $334,147.00. Change order 1 was a total of $25,223.16,
bringing the contract total to $359,370.16. Change Order 2 will increase JS Construction Services
contract by $180,000.00 to a total of $539,370.16. A contingency of 3% is requested of $16,181.10 to
bring the total to $555,551.26. This will be funded from utility tax credits.
Discussion: To view the full discussion click the link below.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/154011
Motion: It Was Moved by Council Member Passiment, Seconded by Council Member Howard to Approve
the Recommendation to Approve the Change Order 2 To Myrtle Park Commercial Site Development (IFB
021121e). Motion Approved Without Objection.
Status: Forward to County Council for approval.
