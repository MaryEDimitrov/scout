Real public records from Beaufort County, South Carolina: a year of Council and committee minutes (October 2025 to October 2026), dated snapshots of the county's live capital projects dashboard, and the county's 2025-2026 Capital Improvement Plan (written in 2024).

- Minutes of County Council and its committees (Public Facilities; Finance, Administration, and Economic
  Development; Executive; Community Services and Public Safety; Natural Resources), plus Council workshops
  and budget workshops. Committees recommend; County Council approves, often as a group on the
  "consent agenda".
- Capital projects dashboard snapshots (`project_list_<date>`): the county replaced its dated project list
  PDFs with a live online dashboard that keeps no past versions, so the app saves dated copies of it.
  Each row has the project's current budget, amount spent to date, status, phase, contractor, projected
  dates, the date the county last updated it, and a latest comment. Compare snapshots to see changes.
- Capital Improvement Plan for fiscal years 2025-2026 (`cip_fy2025-2026_p7-29`): the county's plan,
  written before July 1, 2024, listing projects and their PLANNED cost by fiscal year. Only pages 7-29 of
  the 307-page plan (project lists and summary tables) are included; page N here is page N+6 of the
  original. A planned cost that differs from a later dashboard budget is a change worth checking, but a
  plan is an estimate: scope may have changed, so look for the Council action that explains it.
- Changes also show up when the same project, contract, or dollar figure appears in more than one
  meeting with a different amount, scope, or status.
- Caucus meetings (mostly closed-session notices) are not included.
- This is every posted set of minutes for the listed bodies in the period, not a hand-picked selection.
