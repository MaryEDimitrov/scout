# Reference leads for the real Beaufort County data

Found by hand and reviewed by the project author (former local government reporter) on 2026-10-24,
before any app code was written. This is the yardstick for judging the app's output on the
real documents. Every figure below was checked against the source passage.

Search coverage: all Council and committee agendas and minutes from Dec 2020 to Apr 2022 (CivicClerk),
plus selected backup packets. **Council records from Apr to Nov 2020 are not online anywhere we found**,
so "no approval on record" always means "not in the Dec 2020 to Apr 2022 records."
