Public Comment Card
Rules for Speaking
A. speakers participating in person shall read and fully complete a Public Comment Card
and submit it to the Board's Executive Assistant. Public Comment Cards will be available
at the Board's meeting site beginning thirty (30) minutes before the scheduled Board
meeting begins. Person will be heard in the order Public Comment cards are provided to
the Board's Executive Assistant.
B. Requests to participate in public comments via phone will be accepted electronically.
You can find the BOE Public Comment Form on our website
https://www.beaufortschools.net/about-us/board-of-ed/public-comments Please sign
up using the Public Comment form 24hrs from the day of the meeting. Requestors will
receive a phone call during their respective public forum time and should be ready to
speak.
C. Issues involving pending litigation are not subjects for public comment participation.
D. the use of physically threatening actions or remarks, comments or conduct by
speakers or members of the audience are prohibited, and any behavior that willfully
disrupts a meeting to the extent that the orderly conduct of the meeting is seriously
compromised is prohibited.
E. No presentation by a speaker shall exceed three (3) minutes. The Chair will strictly
enforce this time limit. A suitable timing device shall be employed to notify the speaker of
the end of his/her comment period. Speakers shall not yield their time to another speaker.
F. Persons appearing before the Board should be aware that Board members are without
authority to act independently as individuals in official matters. Neither the Board nor
individual members will respond to speakers during public comment.
G. The Board Chair will designate a location for public comment. Persons shall remain in
such location during their allotted time for public comment. Documents to be provided to
the Board by a public commenter shall be provided to the Board's Executive Assistant
following the commenter's appearance. Persons who have not had the opportunity to
deliver comments publicly may submit written public comments to the Board's Executive
Assistant.

Any person may address the Board for a maximum of three minutes on issues within the
domain of the Board. Each speaker must fill out a public Comment Card.
Beaufort County Board of Education
Public Comment Card
Rules for Speaking
Speakers addressing the Board must:
 Confine comments to issues within the domain of the Board
 Refrain from racial comments, obscenities, and vulgarities, shall not reference or
make derogatory comments about specific individuals by name, and shall commit
other breaches of respect. Speakers may reference names of specific Board
Members, but such communications will not include gossip, defamatory words, or
abusive and vulgar language.
 Not exceed three minutes (visible/audible timing device to be used) (Persons may
not yield time to one another)
 Complete this card, including providing one or more specific topics, and submit it
to the Board Administrative Assistant prior to the conclusion of this period for
public comment
At the request of the presiding officer, individuals who violate the expectations of the
Board during Public Comment will be asked to cease speaking and return to their seats
or to leave the Board Meeting. Should the participant fail to abide by the reasonable
directive of the presiding officer, the Board may prohibit the offending speaker from
participating in further Public Comment sessions for a period not to exceed six months.

12.5 MBK Amendment No 09 (HHIHS Phase 1 Re-Roofing)
This amendment is for the Phase 1 Reroofing Scope of Work. Phase 1 includes
providing all labor, materials, equipment, safety measures, and hoisting to
complete a full roof replacement, including removal and disposal of existing
roofing to expose LWIC decks as outlined in the roofing plan included with the
proposal. The work consists of installing new polyisocyanurate insulation,
including infill areas and transitions, along with multiple roof hatches and rail
systems.
I move that the Board of Education approve Amendment #09 with M.B. Kahn Construction
Co., Inc. for the Phase 1 Reroofing of Hilton Head Island High School for a Guaranteed
Maximum Price (GMP) of $2,201,991.00.
