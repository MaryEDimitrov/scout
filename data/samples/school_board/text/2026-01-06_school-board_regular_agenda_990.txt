Public comments are limited to 3 minutes per speaker on BCSD and school-related topics. Citizens
wishing to participate in public comments can do so in-person or via phone.
There are two scheduled public comment forums during each board meeting. The first is a 30-minute
session near the beginning of the meeting, limited to topics on the Board Meeting Agenda. The second
is a 15-minute session which takes place prior to adjournment, but no later than 9p.m.
Requests to participate in public comments via phone will be accepted electronically. You can find the
BOE Public Comment Form on our website https://www.beaufortschools.net/about-us/board-of-
ed/public-comments Please sign up using the Public Comment form 24hrs from the day of the meeting.
Requestors will receive a phone call during their respective public forum time and should be ready to
speak.
Citizens desiring to participate in-person must fill out a Public Comment Card that can be obtained
from the Board Clerk upon arrival.

3. Public Comments
3.1 Public may address the Board
Any person may address the Board for a maximum of three minutes
on issues within the domain of the Board. Each speaker must fill out a
public Comment Card.
Beaufort County Board of Education
Public Comment Card
Rules for Speaking
A. speakers participating in person shall read and fully complete a
Public Comment Card and submit it to the Board's Executive
Assistant. Public Comment Cards will be available at the Board's
meeting site beginning thirty (30) minutes before the scheduled
Board meeting begins. Person will be heard in the order Public
Comment cards are provided to the Board's Executive Assistant.
B. Requests to participate in public comments via phone will be
accepted electronically. You can find the BOE Public Comment Form
on our website https://www.beaufortschools.net/about-us/board-of-
ed/public-comments Please sign up using the Public Comment form
24hrs from the day of the meeting. Requestors will receive a phone
call during their respective public forum time and should be ready to
speak.
C. Issues involving pending litigation are not subjects for public
comment participation.
D. the use of physically threatening actions or remarks, comments or
conduct by speakers or members of the audience are prohibited, and
any behavior that willfully disrupts a meeting to the extent that the
orderly conduct of the meeting is seriously compromised is
prohibited.
E. No presentation by a speaker shall exceed three (3) minutes. The
Chair will strictly enforce this time limit. A suitable timing device shall

=== Page 3 ===
be employed to notify the speaker of the end of his/her comment
period. Speakers shall not yield their time to another speaker.
F. Persons appearing before the Board should be aware that Board
members are without authority to act independently as individuals in
official matters. Neither the Board nor individual members will
respond to speakers during public comment.
G. The Board Chair will designate a location for public comment.
Persons shall remain in such location during their allotted time for
public comment. Documents to be provided to the Board by a public
commenter shall be provided to the Board's Executive Assistant
following the commenter's appearance. Persons who have not had
the opportunity to deliver comments publicly may submit written
public comments to the Board's Executive Assistant.

Beaufort County Board of Education
Public Comment Card
Rules for Speaking
Speakers addressing the Board must:
 Confine comments to issues within the domain of the Board
 Refrain from racial comments, obscenities, and vulgarities,
shall not reference or make derogatory comments about
specific individuals by name, and shall commit other breaches
of respect. Speakers may reference names of specific Board
Members, but such communications will not include gossip,
defamatory words, or abusive and vulgar language.
 Not exceed three minutes (visible/audible timing device to be
used) (Persons may not yield time to one another)
 Complete this card, including providing one or more specific
topics, and submit it to the Board Administrative Assistant
prior to the conclusion of this period for public comment
At the request of the presiding officer, individuals who violate the
