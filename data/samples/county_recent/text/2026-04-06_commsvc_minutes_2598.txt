The vote at Community Services and Public Safety Committee on February 3, 2026 - 6:2

Motion: It was moved by Council Member Passiment, seconded by Council Member Dawson to
approve 2026/06 An Ordinance adding a new Section 54-8 to the Beaufort County Code of Ordinances
so as to prohibit and make unlawful the discharge of firearms in certain unincorporated areas of the
county, to provide the penalty pertaining to such violations, and to provide certain exceptions and
limitations thereto.
Motion to Amend: It was moved by Council Member Passiment, seconded by Committee Member
Tabernik to amend having paragraph c.5. removed in its entirety and under penalties state a person who
violates any provision of this article shall pay a civil penalty of no more than $500 for each penalty, and
require them to attend a firearms safety course.
The Vote - Voting Yea: Committee Chair Bartholomew, Committee Vice-Chair Reitz, Committee
Member Glover, Committee Member McElynn, Committee Member Tabernik, Ex-Officio Howard,
Council Member Dawson, Council Member Lawson, and Council Member Passiment. The motion to
amend passed 9:0.
Main Motion: It was moved by Council Member Passiment, seconded by Council Member Dawson to
approve 2026/06 An Ordinance adding a new Section 54-8 to the Beaufort County Code of Ordinances
so as to prohibit and make unlawful the discharge of firearms in certain unincorporated areas of the
county, to provide the penalty pertaining to such violations, and to provide certain exceptions and
limitations thereto as amended.
The Vote - Voting Yea: Committee Chair Bartholomew, Committee Vice-Chair Reitz, Committee
Member Glover, Committee Member McElynn, Committee Member Tabernik, Ex-Officio Howard,
Council Member Dawson, Council Member Lawson, and Council Member Passiment. The main motion
passed 9:0.
