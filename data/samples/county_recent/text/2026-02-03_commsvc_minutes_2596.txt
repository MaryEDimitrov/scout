8. Discussion/Presentation Items
a. Discussion and Presentation of a Hate Crime Ordinance - Risa Prince, Lowcountry Coalition
Against Hate
Motion: It was moved by Committee Member McElynn, seconded by Council Member Dawson to
recommend we forward this to Council for full consideration of moving forward by directing the County
Administrator and County Attorney to come forward with a hate intimidation bill that mirrors the one that
has been produced by Richland County.
The Vote - Voting Yea: Committee Chair Bartholomew, Committee Vice-Chair Reitz, Committee
Member Glover, Committee Member McElynn, Committee Member Tabernik, Ex-Officio Howard,
Council Member Dawson, and Council Member Passiment. The motion passed 8:0.
