6. Chief Financial Officer Update
a. FY 2027 Budget Preparation Update
Pinky Harriott, Chief Financial Officer, presented a presentation updating Council with the calculation
of a mil, and reviewing the general fund.
7. FY 2027 Outside Agency Budget Request Presentations
a. Lowcountry Council of Governments Budget Request Presentation - Hank Amundson, Executive
Director
Hank Amundson, Executive Director, presented a presentation of the LOCOG budget request. They
are asking for $295,000 total.
b. Beaufort Memorial Hospital Budget Request Presentation - Russell Baxley, President & CEO
Russell Baxley, President & CEO, presented a presentation of the BMH budget request. They are
asking for the same as the previous year.
c. Beaufort Jasper Hampton Comprehensive Health Services, Inc. Budget Request Presentation - Dr.
Faith Polkey, CEO
Dr. Faith Polkey, CEO, presented a presentation of the BJHCHS budget request.
d. Technical College of the Lowcountry Budget Request Presentation - Mary Lee Carns, Vice
President for Advancement and Workforce Development
Mary Lee Carns, Vice President for Advancement and Workforce Development, presented a
presentation of the TCL budget request. They are asking for the same as last year.
e. University of South Carolina Beaufort Budget Request Presentation - Dr. Al Panu, Chancellor and
Landon Jones, Vice Chancellor of Finance and Operations
Dr. Al Panu, Chancellor, and Landon Jones, Vice Chancellor of Finance and Operations, presented a
presentation of the USCB budget request. They are asking for an increase from 1.1 mils to 1.4 mils.
f. Beaufort Convention and Visitors Bureau Budget Request Presentation - Robb Wells,
President/CEO, Greater Beaufort Port Royal CVB
Rob Wells, President/ CEO, Greater Beaufort Port Royal CVB presented a presentation of the Beaufort
Convention and Visitors budget request.
g. Hilton Head Island Chamber of Commerce Budget Request Presentation - Ariana Pernice, Vice
President, Visitor & Convention Bureau, Hilton Head Island-Bluffton Chamber of Commerce
Ariana Pernice, Vice President, Visitor & Convention Bureau, Hilton Head Chamber of Commerce
presented a presentation of the HHI Chamber of Commerce budget request.
