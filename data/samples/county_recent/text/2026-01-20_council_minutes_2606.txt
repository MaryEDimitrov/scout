a. Under S.C. Code section 30-4-70(a)(2) legal advice related to discussion of negotiations incident
to pending litigation or other matters covered by the attorney client privilege (Update related to
settlement terms of Hatcher Holdings v. Beaufort County (Prospect Road on Daufuskie Island).
7. End of Executive Session
a. Matters arising out of Executive Session
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Lawson, seconded by Council Member Reitz that Council
direct the County Attorney to inform the parties in the Prospect Road Condemnation cases and related
Challenge Actions that Council did not authorize a revision to the Settlement terms and that Council
does not agree to or consent to the revised terms of the proposed Settlement Agreement as discussed
in executive session; and that Beaufort County is to proceed with the Condemnation cases and
Challenge Actions, unless the settlement terms as approved by Council in March of 2024 remain in
effect and are agreed upon by all parties.
The Vote - Voting Yea: Chair Howard, Vice-Chair Tabernik, Council Member Bartholomew, Council
Member Brown, Council Member Dawson, Council Member Glover, Council Member Lawson, Council
Member Passiment, and Council Member Reitz.
Voting Nay: Council Member McElynn. The motion passed 9:1.
