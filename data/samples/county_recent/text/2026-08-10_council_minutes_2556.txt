13. Ordinances for First Reading:
a. 2026/41: An Ordinance amending the Community Development Code (CDC): section 5.3.20
(applicability), 4.2.20 (standards for free-standing accessory buildings/structures), 5.8.100
(screening), and 10.1.190 (definitions).
The vote at Natural Resources Committee on August 3, 2026 - 7:0
Motion: It was moved by Council Member Passiment, seconded by Council Member Glover to approve
First Reading of Beaufort County Ordinance 2026/41 for the following reasons: it is consistent with the
goals, objectives, and policies of the comprehensive plan; it is not in conflict with any provision of this
development code or the code of ordinances; it is not required by changed conditions; it does address a
demonstrated community need; it is consistent with the purpose and intent of the zones in this
development code, or would improve compatibility among uses and ensure efficient development within
the county; it would result in a logical and orderly development pattern; and it would not result in adverse
impacts on the natural environment including but not limited to water, air, noise, stormwater
management, wildlife, vegetation, wetlands, and the natural functioning of the environment.
The Vote - Voting Yea: Chair Howard, Vice-Chair Tabernik, Council Member Bartholomew, Council
Member Cunningham, Council Member Dawson, Council Member Glover, Council Member Lawson,
Council Member McElynn, Council Member Passiment, and Council Member Reitz. The motion passed
