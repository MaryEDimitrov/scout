5. Public Comment Period
No public comments were taken.
6. Chief Financial Officer Update
Chief Financial Officer Pinky Harriott explained to council whom they would be hearing from this
morning and urged them to keep in mind the directives they have already given administration to fund
a COLA, the step program, retiree gap insurance, and the detention center pay band. Please watch
the video stream on the county's website to view the full update
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
7. FY 2027 Elected Officials Budget Request Presentations
a. 14th Circuit Solicitor's Office Budget Request - Solicitor Duffie Stone
14th Circuit Solicitor Duffie Stone presented his office's FY27 budget request, asking Beaufort County
to increase its per capita to $20.89. Please watch the video stream on the county's website to view the
full request. https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
b. Beaufort County Sheriff's Office Budget Request - Beaufort County Sheriff P.J. Tanner
Suzanne Cook, Chief Financial Officer for the Beaufort County Sheriff's Office, presented the Sheriff's
FY 27 Budget request, which is an 8.34% increase from FY26. Please watch the video stream on the
county's website to view the full request. https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
c. Budget Requests from Other Elected Officials
Beaufort County Auditor David Cadd presented his office's FY27 Budget Request, asking County
Council for a 2.51% increase to cover the printing costs that were taken from his budget without his
knowledge.
Probate Judge Heather Galvin presented her office's FY27 Budget Request asking County Council for
a 1.43% decrease in her budget from FY26. She mentioned working to expand the Hilton Head Island
office to better serve the public.
Beaufort County Master in Equity Jim Wegman presented his office's FY27 Budget Request, asking
County Council for a 1.27% budget increase from FY26 to help improve the AV system in their office
and courtroom.
Please watch the video stream on the county's website to view the full requests.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
8. FY 2027 Outside Agency Budget Request Presentations
a. Keep Beaufort County Beautiful Budget Presentation - Gene Zapfel, KBCB Chair
Keep Beaufort County Beautiful Chair Gene Zapfel presented their FY27 Budget Request asking
County Council for a 360% increase to cover the cost of new signage, a rice trunk memorial to honor
the historical aspect rice trunks play in our heritage, recycling initiatives, and new plantings. Please
watch the video stream on the county's website to view the full request.
https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
b. Small Business Association Budget Presentation - Martin Goodman, Area Manager
Beaufort Area Small Business Association Manager Martin Goodman presented the FY27 Budget
Request, asking County Council for a 0% increase from FY26. Please watch the video stream on the
county's website to view the full request. https://beaufortcountysc.new.swagit.com/videos/381851
