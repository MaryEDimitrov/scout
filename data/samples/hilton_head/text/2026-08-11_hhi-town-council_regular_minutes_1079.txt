a separate agreement with the SCDOT to spell out each step of the process. Council also
discussed that Project funding responsibilities remain undetermined at this point, and SCDOT,
the Town, or a combination may ultimately finance the improvements and acknowledged the
existence of traffic challenges, past design objections, and limited options for major changes to
the circle. Council confirmed that public input will be incorporated once early design materials
are available and that the Project remains a priority in ongoing transportation planning and
sales tax referendum considerations.

Consideration of a Resolution Authorizing the Town Manager to Execute and Deliver an
Amended Stormwater Management and Utility Intergovernmental Agreement between the
Town of Hilton Head Island and Beaufort County, South Carolina - Shawn Gillen, Assistant
Town Manager
Sean Gillen, Assistant Town Manager, provided a summary.
The original Stormwater Management and Utility Intergovernmental Agreement (IGA) was
executed in 2001 and established a cooperative partnership between Beaufort County and the
Town of Hilton Head Island, to participate in the countywide Stormwater Management Utility
(Utility) operated by the County. The 2001 IGA was amended and extended in 2006 and again
in 2016. The 2016 IGA expired on June 30, 2026. The 2026 IGA under consideration is
substantively the same terms and conditions established in the previous IGAs, specifically, the
2026 IGA is for a 10 year term with the option to be extended and includes a 180-day written
notice requirement for termination at the end of any given fiscal year. The 2026 IGA reaffirms
Town of Hilton Head Island Town Council

=== Page 8 ===
the terms, conditions, and operational framework for the Utility; and reasserts how the Town
will collaboratively manage stormwater infrastructure, enhance water quality, and meet state
and federal regulatory mandates. The Town maintains full authority to establish its local
stormwater service fee rates each year and is responsible for submitting the approved rates to
Beaufort County by June 30. The County’s administrative fee is a fixed, per-account charge
and does not vary based on the fee assessed to individual properties.
The 2026 IGA reestablishes the Beaufort County Stormwater Implementation Committee,
which is responsible for reviewing regulatory amendments, recommending administrative
budget adjustments, and providing technical guidance on jointly scoped projects shared
between the County and the Town. While Town-led initiatives - such as the Stormwater Master
Plan - identify and prioritize system-wide capital needs, the Stormwater Implementation
Committee provides the formal structure through which the Town and County coordinate the
delivery of projects that cross jurisdictional boundaries or offer mutual benefit to both
jurisdictions.
The Amended Stormwater Management and Utility Intergovernmental Agreement was
considered by the Finance and Administrative Committee on July 13, 2026 and the Committee
voted unanimously to forward the Agreement to Town Council for consideration.
Ms. Becker moved to approve. Ms. Brison seconded.
Mayor Perry asked for public comment. There was none.
Council requested clarification as to how the IGA would be affected as a result of the
Stormwater Master Plan whereby the Town is considering taking ownership of certain local
roads to improve stormwater service and control over repairs, and Mr. Gillen replied that he did
not think there would be an impact on such decisions and pointed-out that the IGA outlines
through the Beaufort County Stormwater Implementation Committee, how the Town and
Beaufort County would jointly handle a project if the location crossed jurisdictional boundaries,
or if there would be shared resources, or if it was mutually beneficial, and if the Town took
more stormwater infrastructure into its jurisdiction, there would actually be less opportunity for
the facilities to cross jurisdictional boundaries.
Motion carried 7-0.
